Retail Sales Analysis — Business Insights Report

1. Project Overview

The objective of this project was to analyze retail sales performance across regions, products, and months using Excel and Microsoft Power BI. An interactive dashboard was developed to monitor revenue, compare regional and product performance, and explore monthly revenue trends.

2. Tools and Technologies

* Microsoft Excel
* Microsoft Power BI
* DAX measures
* Data visualization
* Business analysis

3. Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard includes three primary KPIs:

* Total Revenue:₹142,437.14
* Total Sales Records:240
* Average Sale Value: Approximately ₹593.49, based on the displayed KPI

4. Key Business Findings

Finding 1: Regional Revenue Performance

The Central region generated the highest revenue, totaling **₹30,787.94**.

This identifies Central as the strongest region by revenue in the analyzed dataset.

Finding 2: Product Revenue Performance

Laptop Stand was the highest-revenue product, generating ₹18,713.90.

This indicates that Laptop Stand made a substantial contribution to sales revenue among the products analyzed.

Finding 3: Monthly Revenue Performance

June was the strongest month, generating ₹36,723.17, while February was the weakest month, generating ₹13,788.89.

The difference in revenue was ₹22,934.28. June revenue was approximately 166.3% higher than February revenue, or about 2.66 times as high.

5. Business Recommendations

1. Improve regional performance: Study the Central region and compare its sales patterns with those of other regions.
2. Optimize product planning: Monitor Laptop Stand demand and maintain stock levels that reflect actual sales patterns.
3. Investigate monthly variation: Compare June and February transactions to identify the factors associated with higher and lower revenue.
4. Monitor performance regularly: Use the Power BI dashboard to track regional, product-level, and monthly revenue.
5. Extend the analysis: Examine profit margins, quantities sold, average order value, and customer behavior where the data is available.

6. Conclusion

This project demonstrates how retail sales data can be transformed into an interactive Power BI dashboard and used to identify business patterns.

The analysis identified Central as the highest-revenue region, Laptop Stand as the highest-revenue product, June as the strongest month, and February as the weakest month. These findings provide a starting point for further investigation and data-informed decisions about regional performance, product planning, and sales strategy.
