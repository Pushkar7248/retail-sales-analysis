PRODUCT PERFORMANCE BUSINESS INSIGHTS

Project: Retail Sales Analysis
Period: January–June 2026

Objective
Compare product units sold, total sales revenue, and average sale value.

1. Laptop Stand — 133 units; revenue 18,713.90; average sale 779.75
2. Webcam 1080p — 122 units; revenue 16,809.84; average sale 600.35

7. Ergonomic Chair — 98 units; revenue 10,624.93; average sale 442.71
8. Desk Lamp — 71 units; revenue 10,445.80; average sale 696.39

10. 27-inch Monitor — 76 units; revenue 8,509.33; average sale 472.74
11. USB-C Hub — 77 units; revenue 8,082.10; average sale 505.13
12. Standing Desk — 65 units; revenue 6,526.55; average sale 407.91

Key Findings
1. Laptop Stand generated the highest total revenue (18,713.90) and had the highest reported unit sales (133).
2. Webcam 1080p ranked second by total revenue at 16,809.84.
3. Standing Desk had the lowest total revenue (6,526.55), lowest units sold (65), and lowest average sale value (407.91) among the listed products.
4. Desk Lamp had a relatively high average sale value (696.39) despite 71 units sold.
5. Revenue and unit sales alone do not show which products are most profitable.

Business Recommendations
- Review stock availability and demand patterns for Laptop Stand and Webcam 1080p.
- Investigate the lower sales of Standing Desk before making inventory or promotional decisions.
- Consider product costs, discounts, and margins before recommending products based on profitability.
