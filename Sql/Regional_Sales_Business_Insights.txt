REGIONAL SALES BUSINESS INSIGHTS

Project: Retail Sales Analysis
Period: January–June 2026

Objective
Compare sales records, total revenue, and average sale value across the five regions.

Correct Regional Results (sorted by total revenue)
1. Central — 50 records; revenue 30,787.94; average sale 615.76; revenue contribution approximately 21.62%
2. South — 50 records; revenue 30,083.51; average sale 601.67; revenue contribution approximately 21.12%
3. North — 52 records; revenue 28,925.00; average sale 556.25; revenue contribution approximately 20.31%
4. East — 42 records; revenue 26,485.82; average sale 630.62; revenue contribution approximately 18.59%
5. West — 46 records; revenue 26,154.87; average sale 568.58; revenue contribution approximately 18.36%

Total: 240 records; total revenue 142,437.14.

Key Findings
1. Central generated the highest total revenue (30,787.94), contributing approximately 21.62% of total revenue.
2. South ranked second in revenue at 30,083.51.
3. North had the most records (52) but ranked third by total revenue.
4. East had the fewest records (42) but a comparatively high average sale value.
5. West generated the lowest total revenue (26,154.87).

Business Recommendations
- Review the sales practices, product mix, and customer demand associated with Central's leading revenue.
- Investigate East's average sale value alongside its lower record count.
- Examine West's results to understand whether lower revenue reflects fewer records, lower average sale values, or product mix.
- Compare results over additional periods before treating these differences as long-term regional trends.
