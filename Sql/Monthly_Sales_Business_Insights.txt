MONTHLY SALES BUSINESS INSIGHTS

Project: Retail Sales Analysis
Period: January–June 2026

Objective
Analyse monthly sales revenue, record counts, and average sale value to identify strong and weak months.

Monthly Results
January 2026: 40 records; revenue 24,177.45; average sale 604.44
February 2026: 29 records; revenue 13,788.89; average sale 475.48
March 2026: 39 records; revenue 19,390.28; average sale 497.19
April 2026: 43 records; revenue 23,471.38; average sale 545.85
May 2026: 39 records; revenue 24,885.97; average sale 638.10
June 2026: 50 records; revenue 36,723.17; average sale 734.46

Key Findings
1. June 2026 had the highest monthly revenue: 36,723.17.
2. February 2026 had the lowest monthly revenue: 13,788.89.
3. June also had the highest number of records (50) and the highest average sale value (734.46).
4. Revenue increased by approximately 47.6% from May to June.
5. Total revenue across the six months was 142,437.14.

Business Recommendations
- Investigate what contributed to June's strong results, including product mix, order quantities, customer demand, and sales activity.
- Review February's lower revenue to determine whether it was caused by fewer transactions, smaller sale values, or other factors.
- Compare the same months across future periods before treating these results as a recurring seasonal pattern.
