Retail Sales Analysis Project

1. Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales data to understand business performance, identify sales trends, evaluate product revenue, compare regional performance, and assess salesperson performance.

The objective is to convert raw sales data into meaningful business insights that can support data-driven decisions.

2. Business Objectives

* Calculate key sales performance indicators (KPIs).
* Identify the highest- and lowest-performing regions.
* Analyze revenue and units sold by product.
* Understand monthly sales trends.
* Evaluate salesperson performance.
* Check the quality of the dataset.
* Create visualizations and export reports.

3. Tools and Technologies

4. Dataset Information

The dataset contains retail sales records with the following columns:

* order_id

* product
* quantity

6. Key Findings

* Central was the highest-performing region by revenue.
* West was the lowest-performing region by revenue.
* Laptop Stand was the highest-performing product by revenue.
* June 2026 was the strongest month by revenue.
* February 2026 was the weakest month by revenue.
* The correlation between quantity and total sales revenue was approximately 0.698.
* The data quality checks found no missing values, duplicate records, negative quantities, or negative revenue in the checks performed.

8. Business Value

This analysis helps a business understand which regions and products generate the most revenue, identify weaker sales periods, compare salesperson performance, and monitor key performance indicators.

These findings can support decisions about sales strategy, product performance, and regional planning.
